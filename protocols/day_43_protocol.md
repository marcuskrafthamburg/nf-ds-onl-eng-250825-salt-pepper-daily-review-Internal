## __Required Deliverables__
The primary deliverable is a Notebook with EDA/Modelling, which must include:

* Explanation of the task (you can repeat the text from the challenge).
* Code and visualizations (viz).
* Everything documented.
* Conclusions.
* All data questions/tasks mentioned in the challenge should be tackled.



## __Presentation/Review Rules__
* Each person gets 3 minutes to scroll through their work.

* Any graph that is not interpreted in text within the document cannot be talked about during the review. This simulates the limited time the company will allocate to browse over the work.

## __Key Takeaways__

* The "Vega Rule" of Documentation:
If it’s not written, it doesn’t exist — so your documentation must be precise enough for others to understand and evaluate your work without you.

* Deliverable Priority:
Your main goal is to create a clear, self-explanatory notebook containing the task, all code, visuals, and conclusions for each question.

* The 3-Minute Scrutiny:
Your work should be understandable within 3 minutes; every graph or analysis must have an explanation, or it will be ignored.
