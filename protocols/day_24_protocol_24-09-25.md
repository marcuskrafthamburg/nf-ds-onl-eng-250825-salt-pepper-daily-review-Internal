## __Retrospective__

### About Scrum Retrospectives
**Scrum** is an agile framework for managing product development. The **retrospective** is a key ceremony where the team reflects on their process to continuously improve.

**Purpose of Retrospectives:**
- Reflect on what went well and what didn't
- Identify concrete improvements for the next sprint/period
- Foster team communication and learning culture
- Create actionable change rather than just discussion

### Our Retrospective Structure

**1. Start/Stop/Continue Notes** 📝
- **Start**: What should we begin doing?
- **Stop**: What should we stop doing? 
- **Continue**: What's working well that we should keep doing?

**2. Voting Phase** 🗳️
- Team members vote on the most important topics
- Focus discussion on high-priority items

**3. Discussion & Action Items** 💬
- Discuss topics with most votes
- Define concrete measures and actions
- Assign responsibilities where needed
